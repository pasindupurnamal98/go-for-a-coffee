A story, five years in the making

Some songs
just hit differently
after certain moments.

This is one of them. A small archive of everything that led here — a campus, a five-year gap, and a night at a concert that changed the frequency.

Chapter 01

The Timeline

  • Five years ago

    Matrix Campus

    We first met — briefly. We didn't become close. Life moved on and we went our separate ways, the way people do when the timing just isn't right yet.

  • Exactly five years later

    Chakra360 concert

    Unexpectedly, we met again. Same two people, but everything about the moment felt different this time.

  • That night

    The middle of a huge outdoor crowd

    My first time experiencing his concert like that — not backstage, not indoors, just in the crowd, in the sound. It became unforgettable, and I don't think that was only about the music.

  • Afterward

    "Some songs just hit differently..."

    That was the first message. You replied warmly, and somehow that turned into one of the most honest conversations I'd had in a long time.

Chapter 02

What Stayed With Me

"Music creates memories. Every song reminds me of people and moments. I still remember everything." — you, on why the songs mattered

I told you that every time I hear those songs now, I remember every detail — I feel every emotion again. And you understood that immediately, which is rarer than it sounds.

"Definitely, I won't forget." · "You're gonna make me cry." · "I felt the same." — the replies that made it real

Then there were the rainy days — I joked it was maybe because of them. You teased back: "So if it wasn't raining? 😌" — and somehow that one line became its own small memory.

"It reminded me of you." — the reel you sent, about people who text after hanging out just to say they had a great time
Chapter 03

Her Words, Not Mine

I shared a few songs with you. What you sent back meant more to me than the songs themselves ever could.

Chapter 04

Habit

Chapter 05

A Letter, Instead of a Text

I could have just sent you a message. But some things deserve more care than a text can hold, so I built this instead.

This isn't only about romance. It's about reconnecting after years apart, discovering there might be something deeper here than either of us expected, and how music somehow kept a place open for this moment the whole time.

Thank you for the conversations, the honesty, and for sending me that reel without overthinking it. It meant more than you probably know.

— written on a rainy day, on purpose
